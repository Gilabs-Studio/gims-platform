# Product

## Register

product

## Users

GIMS is used by tenant business teams, system administrators, finance operators, HRD teams, sales teams, warehouse teams, and store operators. Users work inside dense operational workflows where accuracy, auditability, and quick recovery matter more than decorative presentation.

## Product Purpose

GIMS is an ERP/POS platform for managing core business operations across tenants: master data, sales, purchases, inventory, finance, HRD, CRM, subscriptions, permissions, and platform administration. Success means users can complete daily operational tasks with fewer manual handoffs, clear permission boundaries, and reliable tenant-level data isolation.

## Brand Personality

Clear, reliable, efficient. The interface should feel competent and calm, with enough structure to support complex work without making routine tasks feel heavy.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer-style landing page aesthetics inside the app, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, oversized rounded cards, over-animated dashboards, and clever custom controls that make standard ERP tasks harder to scan. Avoid hiding operational state behind vague copy or icon-only actions.

## Design Principles

- Task clarity first: every screen should make the next operational action obvious.
- Reliable by default: data states, permissions, errors, and destructive actions must be explicit.
- Dense but readable: support tables and forms without sacrificing hierarchy.
- Familiar controls: use standard dashboard patterns and shared components over custom novelty.
- Permission-aware surfaces: menus and actions reflect backend authorization, but backend remains source of truth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Keep keyboard access for forms, dialogs, tables, and menu actions. Use visible focus states, readable contrast, explicit labels, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and text alternatives for icon actions.
